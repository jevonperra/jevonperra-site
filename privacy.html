Privacy

Last updated October 19, 2026

This is the personal website of Jevon Perra. This page explains what information the site collects and what happens to it.

What is collected

If you send a message with the contact form, the site collects your name, email address and message. The form is delivered by Web3Forms, which passes it to Jevon by email.

If you allow analytics, Google Analytics records anonymized visit data such as pages viewed, device type and approximate location. Analytics stays off until you choose Allow.

How it is used

Messages are used only to reply to you. Analytics data is used only to see which pages people read. Your information is not sold or rented to anyone.

Links to other sites

This site links to YouTube, Spotify, Amazon, LinkedIn, mybridge.loans and perraloans.com. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Do Not Track

The site does not respond to Do Not Track signals. You can turn analytics off with the No thanks button or by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Your choices

To ask what information you have sent or to have it deleted, use the contact form on the home page. California residents may request this under the California Consumer Privacy Act.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change.
