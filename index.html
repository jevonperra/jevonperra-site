About

At five years old I was on my family's job sites in overalls, carrying bricks.

That is where real estate started for me: building and remodeling multifamily properties for my family's business in Southern California. I paid my way through Missouri Southern State University with a commercial window washing company called Mr. Squeegee.

I spent years training leaders and managers in human behavior and communication at companies like Boeing, Kaiser Permanente and Microsoft, and earned coaching, negotiation and Fierce Conversations certifications along the way. I studied at Calvary Chapel School of Ministry.

I have been in lending for more than twenty years, and I teach spiritual principles every chance I get. Today I live in Fountain Valley, California with my family. I have five sisters and two brothers, and I cried like a baby at The Notebook.
